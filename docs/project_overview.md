# STYLECYCLE - Project Overview

## 1. Executive Summary
STYLECYCLE is a smart, circular fashion e-commerce marketplace empowering users to buy, sell, and exchange verified new and pre-owned clothing. By combining a curated marketplace with personalized digital wardrobe management, AI-assisted styling, and proprietary FitMatch size intelligence, STYLECYCLE aims to significantly reduce clothing returns, combat textile waste, and make personal style expressive and affordable.

## 2. Core Pillars
1. **Marketplace (Buy, Sell & Exchange)**: P2P circular fashion economy with verified condition grades, fair pricing, and counter-offers.
2. **FitMatch Intelligence**: Precise sizing engine matching individual measurements against brand-specific dimensional charts.
3. **Digital Wardrobe**: Cloud wardrobe management for cataloging owned apparel by category, season, palette, and wear frequency.
4. **AI Personal Stylist**: Context-aware outfit generator combining user wardrobe pieces with marketplace inventory.
5. **Smart Shopping (Complete Your Wardrobe)**: Gap analysis recommending missing foundational pieces rather than redundant impulse buys.
6. **Admin & Analytics**: Real-time moderation, order settlement, dispute mediation, and ESG sustainability reporting.

## 3. Target Audience
- Conscious consumers, college students, and young professionals.
- Fashion enthusiasts seeking curated vintage and modern designer pieces.
- Sellers seeking hassle-free decluttering and instant wardrobe monetisation.
